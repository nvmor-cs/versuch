Privacy Policy

App: Lumora · Last updated: August 2026 · Deutsch

In short

Lumora has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is a single exception: the food search – more on that below.

What data does the app process?

Everything you enter in the app – workouts, training plans, custom exercises, meals, goals, settings – is stored locally on your device only. It does not leave your device.

Food search (Open Food Facts)

When you search for a food in the nutrition section, the app queries the open database Open Food Facts. Your search term (or the barcode number you typed) is sent to their server; for technical reasons that server also sees your IP address. No identifier that would make you or your device recognisable is sent along, and nothing from your diary is transmitted – neither what you ate nor how much, neither goals nor anything from the training section.

This request only happens while you are actively typing in the search field. If you want to avoid it, search your own list and the built-in basics, or create foods yourself – the app needs no connection for that. Open Food Facts privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Sharing with third parties

Apart from the search request described above, nothing is shared with third parties. The app does not transmit any of your entries anywhere.

Android backup

The app allows Android's automatic backup (“Auto Backup”). If it is switched on for your device, Android – not the app – backs up the data folder including your training and nutrition data to your Google account, and restores it after a reinstall or on a new device. This is the same backup that applies to all apps, and you can switch it off in the Android settings under “Backup”. The app itself does not transmit this data anywhere.

Data export and deletion

In the app settings you can export a backup of your data as a file at any time, or delete all data irreversibly. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Permissions

The app requests neither location nor contacts. It uses a short vibration as tap feedback, internet access for the food search and – if you agree – a silent notification counting down the remaining rest time. There is no sound or vibration at the end of a rest.

Step counter: if you switch on steps in the health section, you grant the app the “Physical activity” permission. It is used to read nothing but the device's own motion sensor, which counts steps anyway. The numbers stay on the device and are not transmitted anywhere. Without the permission – or on a device without that sensor – everything works as before and you enter steps by hand.

Camera: only for the barcode scanner in the nutrition section, and only while it is open. The image is evaluated on the device alone – nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted. The only thing sent out is the recognised sequence of digits, as a search query to Open Food Facts (see above). If you do not grant access, you can type in the number under the barcode instead.
