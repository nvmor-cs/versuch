## Vollständige Beschreibung (max. 4000, hier 2908)

```
Lumora is your training log, your food diary and your health notebook – in one app: dark, calm and free of clutter. No account, no ads, no cloud. Everything stays on your device.

THREE WORLDS, ONE SWIPE
Training, nutrition and health sit side by side. A swipe across the bar at the bottom moves between them – each world has its own tabs and its own storage.

TRAINING
• Log set by set: weight and reps with large plus/minus buttons, one tap completes the set
• Last time's numbers are already there – usually you just confirm them
• Rest timer starts after every set, duration is up to you
• 151 exercises, filtered by muscle group and equipment
• Create your own exercises: weight × reps, reps only or time
• Training plans with several workouts – push / pull / legs, for instance
• Supersets: link two exercises and alternate between them
• New personal bests are spotted automatically
• Volume for the week, quarter, year and all time, plus a progress curve for every exercise

YOUR COACH
On first launch a short walkthrough explains the app. After that your coach can build a training plan for you: it asks about your goal, experience, training days, where you train and how much time you have – and puts together a plan you are free to edit afterwards.

NUTRITION
• Diary for breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
• Daily calorie goal, with protein, carbs and fat as shares of it
• 76 staple foods are included offline, your own are added in seconds
• Scan a barcode or search online (Open Food Facts) – anything found once stays available offline
• Portions instead of arithmetic: "1 slice", "1 tub" – or simply the number of grams

HEALTH
• Weight with a rolling seven-day average: the line shows the trend, not the noise of a single day
• Target weight as a line in the chart, plus measurements for waist, chest, upper arm, thigh and hips
• Training calendar with a weekly goal and a streak – one glance tells you how the month went
• Muscle group balance: completed sets per muscle group next to a rough guideline. It shows what is falling behind without telling you what to do
• 23 daily trackers you pick yourself: creatine, protein shake, vitamin D, magnesium, water, sleep, resting heart rate, energy, mood, soreness and more. Only what you switch on appears on screen
• Steps are counted by the device itself if you allow it – nothing to type in

ENGLISH AND GERMAN
The whole app switches language in the settings, down to the names of exercises and foods.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Everything is stored locally on your device – weight, steps and diary included
• Export a backup as a file and read it back in whenever you like
• Works offline. The only thing that goes online is the food search, and only while you are using it

Lumora is a fitness and wellness app, not a medical device. It makes no diagnoses and is no substitute for medical advice.
```
